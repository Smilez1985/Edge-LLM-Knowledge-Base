# Knowledge packs (contributions)

Pull requests put knowledge packs here: `contribution-knowledge-<id>-<version>.zip`, exported with
`llmcf zoo export knowledge --author "<name>" --pr` or uploaded here in the browser. Every page keeps its source
address and its owner's terms. Only pack files of at most 5 MB; the check workflow runs `llm-cli zoo verify` on each.
After review the maintainer moves an accepted pack to [packs/knowledge](../../packs/knowledge) and adds it to the
signed index. How to contribute: [README](../../README.md#contributing).

Hier legen Pull Requests Wissens-Packs ab; nach dem Review wandert ein angenommenes Pack nach `packs/knowledge/` und in den signierten Index ([README_DE](../../README_DE.md#beitragen)).
